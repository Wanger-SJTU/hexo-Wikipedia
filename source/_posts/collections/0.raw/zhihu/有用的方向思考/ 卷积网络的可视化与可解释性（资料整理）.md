### 论文：

- [2014-ECCV-Visualizing and Understanding Convolutional Networks](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//arxiv.org/abs/1311.2901) 

- **Intro：**对卷积网络不同层的学习情况进行了可视化分析

- [2015-ICLR-Striving for Simplicity: The All Convolutional Net](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//arxiv.org/abs/1412.6806) 

- **Intro：**introduce a new variant of the “deconvolution approach” for visualizing features learned by CNNs 

- 2016-CVPR-Learning Deep Features for Discriminative Localization 

- **Intro：**提出class activation map(CAM)方法，即卷积最后一层的Feature map经过GAP后得到的特征向量通过fc层与类别建立了联系(对特征向量加权得到类别响应值），利用该权重可以对Feature map加权来得到网络关注的空间区域

- [2017-ICCV-Grad-CAM: Visual Explanations from Deep Networks via Gradient-based Localization](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//arxiv.org/abs/1610.02391)

- **Intro：**CAM方法的改进版本，利用梯度信息进行加权，不再依赖于GAP层
- 代码实现：[Pytorch](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//github.com/kazuto1011/grad-cam-pytorch)

- [2018-WACV-Grad-CAM++: Improved Visual Explanations for Deep Convolutional Networks](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//arxiv.org/abs/1710.11063) 

- 代码实现：[Tensorflow](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//github.com/adityac94/Grad_CAM_plus_plus)

- [2018-CVPR-Tell Me Where to Look: Guided Attention Inference Network](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//arxiv.org/abs/1802.10171) 

- **Intro：**在识别船的任务中，如果大量图片背景有水，模型会根据是否有水来对船进行识别，这对模型泛化能力有很大的影响，本文通过分络网络关注区域来引导网络学习正确的特征进而提高模型的泛化能力
- 代码实现：[chainer](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//github.com/alokwhitewolf/Guided-Attention-Inference-Network)

- [arXiv:1812.10025-Attention Branch Network: Learning of Attention Mechanism for Visual Explanation](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//arxiv.org/abs/1812.10025) 

- **Intro：**通过网络关注区域实现类似attention机制的方法来提高学习效果
- 代码实现：[Pytorch](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//github.com/machine-perception-robotics-group/attention_branch_network) 

- [2017-ICLR-Paying More Attention to Attention: Improving the Performance of Convolutional Neural Networks via Attention Transfer](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//arxiv.org/abs/1612.03928) 

- **Intro：**知识蒸馏方法的应用，利用teacher的attention map引导学生网络的学习
- 代码实现：[Pytorch](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//github.com/szagoruyko/attention-transfer) 

- 2017-CVPR-Network Dissection: Quantifying Interpretability of Deep Visual Representations
- [arXiv:1901.02413-Interpretable CNNs](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//arxiv.org/pdf/1901.02413.pdf) 

- **Intro: **相比传统CNN的卷积核可能会对混合的特征激活，本文给高层的卷积核添加filter loss使其对特定的object part响应，使其更有可解释性
