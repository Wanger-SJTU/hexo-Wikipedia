又是一年OSDI，整理一下目前已知的涉及<strong>MLSys</strong>领域的工作，顺序不分先后（持续更新）：

- [Mirage: A Multi-Level Superoptimizer for Tensor Programs](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//arxiv.org/abs/2405.05751)*. Mengdi Wu, Xinhao Cheng, Shengyu Liu, Chunan Shi, Jianan Ji, Kit Ao, Praveen Velliengiri, Xupeng Miao, Oded Padan, and Zhihao Jia*. <strong>From CMU Catalyst & Purdue</strong>
- PipeThreader: Software-Defined Pipeline for Efficient DNN Execution. *Yu Cheng, Lei Wang, Yining Shi, Yuqing Xia, Lingxiao Ma, Jilong Xue, Yang Wang, Zhiwen Mo, Feiyang Chen, Fan Yang, Mao Yang, Zhi Yang. *<strong>From MSRA & PKU</strong>
- KPerfIR: Towards a Open and Compiler-centric Ecosystem for GPU Kernel Performance Tooling on Modern AI Workloads. *Yue Guan, Yuanwei Fang, Keren Zhou, Corbin Robeck, Manman Ren, Zhongkai Yu, Yufei Ding, Adnan Aziz.*<strong>From UCSD PICASSO LAB</strong>
- [WLB-LLM: Workload-Balanced 4D Parallelism for Large Language Model Training.](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//arxiv.org/pdf/2503.17924) *Zheng Wang, Anna Cai, Xinfeng Xie, Zaifeng Pan, Yue Guan, Weiwei Chu, Jie Wang, Shikai Li, Jianyu Huang, Chris Cai, Yuchen Hao, Yufei Ding.*<strong>From UCSD PICASSO LAB</strong>
- Training with Confidence: Catching Silent DL Training Bugs with Automated Proactive Checks. *Yuxuan Jiang, Ziming Zhou, Boyu Xu, Beijie Liu, Runhui Xu, Peng Huang. *<strong>From UMich</strong>
- Enabling Efficient GPU Communication over Multiple NICs with FuseLink. *Zhenghang Ren, Yuxuan Li, Zilong Wang, Xinyang Huang, Wenxue Li, Kaiqiang Xu, Xudong Liao, Yijun Sun, Bowen Liu, Han Tian, Junxue Zhang, Mingfei Wang, Zhizhen Zhong, Guyue Liu, Ying Zhang, Kai Chen. *<strong>From HKUST iSING Lab</strong>
- Understanding Stragglers in Large Model Training using What-if Analysis. *Jinkun Lin et al.* <strong>From NYU & ByteDance</strong>
- Achieving Low-Latency Graph-Based Vector Search via Aligning Best-First Search Algorithm with SSD. *Hao Guo, Youyou Lu. *<strong>From THU Storage Group</strong>
- Quake: Adaptive Indexing for Vector Search. *Jason Mohoney,Devesh Sarda, Mengze Tang, Shihabur Rahman Chowdhury, Anil Pacaci, Ihab F. Ilyas, Theodoros Rekatsinas, Shivaram Venkataraman.* <strong>From UW-Madison</strong>
- [Fast and Live Model Auto Scaling with O(1) Host Caching.](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//arxiv.org/pdf/2412.17246) *Dingyan Zhang, Haotian Wang, Yang Liu, Xingda Wei, Yizhou Shan, Rong Chen, and Haibo Chen. *<strong>From SJTU IPADS</strong>
- [LLMoC: Large Language Model Inference at Wafer Scale.](https://link.zhihu.com/?target=https%3A//arxiv.org/pdf/2502.04563)*Congjie He, Yeqi Huang, Pei Mu, Ziming Miao, Jilong Xue, Lingxiao Ma, Fan Yang, Luo Mai.*<strong>From UoE & MSRA</strong>
- Bayesian Code Diffusion for Efficient Automatic Deep Learning Program Optimization. * Isu Jeong, Seulki Lee. *<strong>From UNIST Embedded AI Lab</strong>
